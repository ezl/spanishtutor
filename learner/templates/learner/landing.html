How we think about learning

Most language apps make you choose what to practice. That's a problem — deciding what to learn is cognitive work, and it's work you shouldn't have to do.

We track your skills across 35 grammar and vocabulary topics, from A1 to C2. After every session, we know what you've mastered, what's slipping, and what's at the edge of your ability.

When you show up, we tell you what's next. Not a menu of options — a decision. We pick the skill most likely to move you forward and get to work.

Sessions can be as short as a few minutes or as long as you like. Either way, every session counts.

The content isn't generic. We use your interests, hobbies, and life — the things you actually care about — to build lessons that feel relevant to you specifically, not a textbook written for everyone and no one.

You can always take the wheel. Say "let's work on subjunctive" or "teach me vocabulary for traveling in Japan" and we'll follow your lead. But you'll never have to.

The goal is simple: remove every friction that isn't the actual learning.
