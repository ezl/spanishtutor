Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Luz Angela Spanish Tutor ("we", "us") is a personalized Spanish learning service delivered via Facebook Messenger, Discord, and the web. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Platform ID — your Messenger PSID or Discord user ID, used to identify you across sessions.
  • Display name — your name as provided by the platform.
  • Messages — the text you send to the tutor, used to generate responses and assess your Spanish level.
  • Skill scores — your assessed performance across Spanish grammar and vocabulary topics (A1–C2).
  • Learning profile — your native language, interests, and goals, collected during onboarding to personalize lessons.

How we use it

  • To provide and improve the tutoring service.
  • To track your progress and personalize lesson content.
  • To send session reminders (if enabled).

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Anthropic — your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate tutor responses. Anthropic Privacy Policy.
  • Meta — if you use Messenger, your messages are delivered via Meta's platform. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Railway — our infrastructure provider, where data is stored. Railway Privacy Policy.

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time.
